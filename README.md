# EvoBreed-ML
### An Integrated Population Genetics, Multi-Omics and Machine Learning Framework for Evolutionary Tracking and Adaptive Trait Prediction

## Project Overview

EvoBreed-ML is an integrated computational research framework designed to combine population genetics, genomics, transcriptomics, molecular genetics, quantitative genetics and machine learning to investigate genetic variation, evolutionary processes and adaptive trait development in crop populations.

The project aims to estimate allele frequencies, gene frequencies, genotype frequencies, genetic diversity, population differentiation and breeding values while investigating how changes in genetic composition across successive generations contribute to phenotypic variation and evolutionary adaptation.

By integrating genomic variation, gene expression profiles, molecular markers and phenotypic observations, EvoBreed-ML provides a comprehensive approach to understanding the genetic mechanisms underlying important agricultural traits, including disease resistance, susceptibility, environmental adaptation, crop productivity and fibre quality.

## Major Objectives

1. Estimate allele frequencies, gene frequencies and genotype frequencies across populations and generations.
2. Calculate population genetic parameters, genetic diversity indices and population differentiation statistics.
3. Investigate evolutionary mechanisms, including natural selection, genetic drift, mutation, migration, gene flow and inbreeding.
4. Integrate genomic variation, molecular markers, haplotypes and transcriptomic information.
5. Identify genes, alleles and molecular features associated with adaptive and economically important traits.
6. Estimate genetic effects, breeding values and genomic estimated breeding values.
7. Develop machine learning models for predicting allele-frequency changes, gene expression, resistance phenotypes and breeding performance.
8. Track evolutionary changes across generations and investigate their association with phenotypic adaptation.
9. Identify genetic signatures associated with the development of resistance and susceptibility.
10. Predict potential evolutionary and breeding outcomes under different selection and environmental scenarios.

## Machine Learning Integration

The framework incorporates machine learning and statistical modelling approaches, including Random Forest, Support Vector Machines, XGBoost, Elastic Net, Logistic Regression, Bayesian Regression and Artificial Neural Networks.

These models are intended to identify important genetic features, predict phenotypic responses, estimate genetic merit and investigate relationships between evolutionary genetic changes and adaptive traits.

## Evolutionary Tracking and Adaptive Trait Analysis

A major component of EvoBreed-ML is the tracking of allele-frequency and genotype-frequency changes across successive generations.

The framework investigates whether increases or decreases in the frequencies of particular alleles are associated with changes in traits such as disease resistance, disease susceptibility, yield and fibre quality.

Evolutionary simulations will be used to compare neutral evolution, positive selection, negative selection, balancing selection, genetic drift and migration.

## Simulated Data and Validation

The project includes large-scale simulated datasets representing multiple populations, generations, individuals, genomic markers, gene-expression profiles and phenotypic traits.

Simulation datasets provide known genetic and evolutionary parameters to evaluate the accuracy of statistical calculations, machine learning models and evolutionary predictions.

The framework is also designed to accept real experimental genotype, phenotype, molecular marker and transcriptomic datasets.

## Programming Languages and Technologies

- Python – Statistical genetics, bioinformatics, machine learning, simulations and visualization.
- Java – Genetic parameter calculations, genotype processing, evolutionary simulation and computational modules.
- R-compatible workflows – Integration with established statistical genetics and transcriptomic tools.
- Machine Learning – Predictive modelling and feature identification.
- Data Visualization – Evolutionary trajectories, genetic diversity plots, population structure, genomic heatmaps and molecular networks.

## Expected Outcomes

EvoBreed-ML aims to provide an integrated computational platform for understanding how genetic variation changes across generations, identifying molecular and genomic features associated with adaptive traits, estimating breeding values and predicting potential evolutionary outcomes.

The ultimate goal is to connect population-level evolutionary processes with molecular mechanisms and practical crop improvement through computational genetics and artificial intelligence.
